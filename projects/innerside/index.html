PROJECT FRAME

Brief, context and operating frame in one place.

This section gathers the brief, constraints, outcomes and public evidence into one readable project frame.

Brief

Help RED DOOR shape its first product into a coherent and testable technical system without prematurely revealing the experience it is preparing.

Context

InnerSide is an emerging software project at the intersection of territory, context and human experience. Its detailed proposition remains deliberately undisclosed while RED DOOR and its collaborators establish the product and technical foundations.

Constraints

  • Keep RED DOOR clearly identified as the incubator and project lead.
  • Describe Electronic Artefacts as a strategic and technology collaborator with an active R&D contribution, not as the project lead.
  • Preserve a deliberately limited public outline until RED DOOR chooses to reveal the product experience.
  • Do not present research prototypes as a public release, market validation or production-ready system.

Outcomes

  • An active strategic and technology collaboration between RED DOOR and Electronic Artefacts.
  • A governed R&D foundation and early prototype work under development.
  • A public record that attributes project leadership and technical contribution without revealing the product experience.

PROJECT InnerSide

PROJECT THESIS

Detailed reading notes.

Only the threshold

InnerSide is RED DOOR’s first product. Its field is territorial, contextual and human; its exact form remains deliberately out of view.

This page documents the existence of the work, the people carrying it and the contribution made by Electronic Artefacts. It is not a reveal, a feature list or a launch announcement.

Carried by RED DOOR

RED DOOR incubates and leads InnerSide. The collective holds the product direction and decides what the project becomes, what remains private and when a wider public view is appropriate.

The Electronic Artefacts contribution

Electronic Artefacts supports RED DOOR as a strategic and technology collaborator. The studio contributes actively to R&D through product and technology counsel, software architecture, technical exploration, prototyping and the development of foundations intended to make the project coherent and durable.

This is a technology contribution to a project carried by RED DOOR. Electronic Artefacts does not present InnerSide as one of its own products.

Current state

The project is in research and prototyping. The work includes a governed product framework, architecture studies and executable experiments, but none of these is presented here as a finished service or a production system.

What remains behind the door

The detailed product experience, its first public use and its release path are intentionally absent from this record. For now, the visible signal is the collaboration itself: RED DOOR is building InnerSide, and Electronic Artefacts is helping give it strategic and technological form.
