## The collective

RED DOOR is an independent product collective and incubator. It carries the direction and development of InnerSide, its first product.

The public outline is intentionally partial. InnerSide is being shaped through product research, system architecture and prototyping; the experience itself remains behind the door for now.

## Collaboration with Electronic Artefacts

Electronic Artefacts acts as a strategic and technology collaborator to RED DOOR. Its contribution includes product and technology counsel, active R&D, architecture work and the development of technical foundations.

The roles remain explicit: RED DOOR incubates and leads the project; Electronic Artefacts contributes technology, research and strategic support without presenting itself as the project lead.

## Current focus

The collaboration is currently in an R&D phase. The work is real and active, but no public release, market validation or finished product is claimed by this record.
